VOICEOVER: Your brief asks for about six months of weekly forecast revisions and accuracy per horizon. Every revision is kept, so for any customer, item and delivery week I can show how the forecast moved as delivery approached. Consumption is scoped by customer, item, site and a four-week window. A forecast of 9.4 and firm orders of 9.0 plan 9.4 units, not 18.4, so demand is never counted twice. Accuracy is scored as weighted absolute percentage error, because a simple percentage error breaks on zero-demand weeks. The shape matters for the policy decision. Inside two weeks the error is about ten percent; beyond eight weeks it exceeds half of actual demand, and it runs high at every horizon. That tells you which forecast horizons a buffer should react to.
